CISA flags exploited N-able MailAssure and MONAI Label flaws

CISA has added vulnerabilities affecting N-able MailAssure and MONAI Label to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, citing evidence of active exploitation. The MailAssure issue involves server-side request forgery in its LDAP functionality; teams using either product should prioritize reviewing the relevant advisories and remediation guidance.
