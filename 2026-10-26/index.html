Critical Wasmtime flaws threaten WebAssembly sandbox isolation

The Hacker News reports two critical Wasmtime vulnerabilities that could allow malicious WebAssembly modules to escape their sandbox. Exploitation requires a vulnerable build to compile an attacker-controlled module, and one flaw affects only ARM64 systems. Teams embedding Wasmtime should review their runtime versions and the maintainers' fixes.
