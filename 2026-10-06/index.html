Google pauses open-source product bug bounty rewards

Google stopped accepting product vulnerability reports for rewards through its open-source software bug bounty program on October 1, following a surge in invalid automated reports. The change affects projects such as Go, Angular and Protocol Buffers, while supply-chain compromise reports remain eligible for submission.
