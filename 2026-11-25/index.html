Rackspace Spot shutdown follows Kubernetes security breach

Rackspace is shutting down its Spot cloud platform after a breach gave attackers access to customer Kubernetes clusters and secrets, according to BleepingComputer. Customers have until December 8 to migrate services and download their data, making migration planning and review of exposed credentials immediate operational concerns.
