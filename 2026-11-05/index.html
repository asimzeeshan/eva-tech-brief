CISA flags active exploitation of a patched Apple flaw

CISA added CVE-2024-27834 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog following reports of active exploitation. The improper-authentication flaw affects multiple Apple operating systems and has a CVSS score of 5.5, illustrating that a medium severity rating does not rule out real-world attacks.

OpenAI reports 948 Linux privilege-escalation results from an AI pipeline

OpenAI described a prototype using an upcoming cyber-focused model to validate and patch 948 Linux local privilege-escalation vulnerabilities, with reported API costs of $1.2 million. The pipeline analyzed 2,308 candidate kernel findings and dynamically reproduced 1,724 vulnerabilities, highlighting the growing role of AI in vulnerability research.
