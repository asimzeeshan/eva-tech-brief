Pakistan-built service robots reach Karachi restaurants

Dawn reports that locally built robots are serving diners at a Karachi restaurant, carrying food between tables and greeting customers. Service robots remain uncommon in Pakistan, making the deployment a concrete example of local robotics moving into everyday commercial use.

Brava Security has emerged from stealth with $15 million in funding. Its multi-agent AI platform generates telemetry resembling an attack to help identify gaps in enterprise defenses, focusing on whether security systems can observe the signals an intrusion would produce.

Nvidia H200 buyer approvals face scrutiny over empty offices

The Verge reports concerns about how the US Commerce Department and Nvidia vet Chinese companies approved to receive H200 AI chips. Cloudflare redesigns D1 control plane for millions of databases

Cloudflare has described a redesign of the control plane for its D1 serverless SQL database to support millions of databases. As reported by InfoQ, the approach avoids centralized bottlenecks and combines retries, idempotency, and eventual consistency, offering a useful distributed-systems example for infrastructure developers.
