California passes open-source OS exemption from age-verification law

California lawmakers unanimously passed Assembly Bill 1856, which exempts open-source operating systems from the state's Digital Age Assurance Act. The exemption covers software distributed under licenses including GPL, MIT, BSD, and Apache ahead of the law's planned January 2027 start.
