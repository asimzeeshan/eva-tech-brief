Check Point zero-day exploited to gain full administrative access

Check Point patched CVE-2026-16232, a critical authentication bypass in the SmartConsole login process that has been exploited in the wild. msaRAT malware hides command traffic inside Chrome and Edge

The Chaos ransomware group is using a newly identified backdoor called msaRAT to conceal command-and-control communications. The malware routes its traffic through Chrome or Edge, making malicious activity harder to distinguish from normal browser use.

AI researchers say Kimi K3's rapid capability gains are unlikely to be explained solely by distillation from Anthropic's Fable model. Their assessment highlights the difficulty of attributing frontier-model performance to a single training technique or alleged source model.
