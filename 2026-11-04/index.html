Barracuda vulnerabilities could combine to grant root access

Barracuda disclosed two vulnerabilities that could be chained to bypass authorization and gain root access on a susceptible device. The reported chain combines a pre-authentication authorization bypass with command injection, making exposed affected appliances a priority for security review.

Chainstack reports infrastructure breach exposing customer data

Blockchain infrastructure provider Chainstack disclosed unauthorized access to parts of its infrastructure, with the report describing exposure of customer data and password hashes. The incident has been assessed as part of a broader AI-powered attack campaign, although the available summary does not establish the full scope of the compromise.

Anthropic develops FlashProve to verify AI model identity

Anthropic is working on FlashProve, a system intended to let users verify that an AI response came from the model it claims to represent. Model provenance could help developers assess the authenticity of responses supplied through intermediaries; the report describes work in progress.
