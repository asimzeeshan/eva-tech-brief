Cisco SD-WAN authentication bypass used to deploy KeyDrift

BleepingComputer reports that the threat group tracked as UAT-11473 exploited CVE-2026-20127 in Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Controllers. The authentication-bypass flaw was used to deploy a custom malware framework called KeyDrift, making controller security directly relevant to network operations teams.

CISA flags exploited GitLab and Cisco firewall flaws

CISA added a GitLab remote-code-execution vulnerability and a Cisco firewall denial-of-service vulnerability to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, according to The Hacker News. The GitLab issue, CVE-2026-97229, is described as an integer overflow or wraparound flaw with a CVSS score of 10.0. The additions signal observed exploitation rather than only theoretical exposure.
